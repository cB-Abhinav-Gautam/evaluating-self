2026Editing a detector without training

A dairy runs ninety-five cameras, every one feeding a small single-stage detector that counts people and vehicles. How far that detector could be improved by editing its weights and its graph, and by a decision layer on top of its boxes, without training anything. The vehicle rows merged into one score, a decision layer that counts what stays, a per-camera cut-off for truck yards, the dairy's own brand found in the search embedding, and four things that did not work. 7 November.
